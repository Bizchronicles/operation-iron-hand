OPERATION IRON HAND

AI-Powered Business Management Platform

Operation Iron Hand is an intelligent business management platform designed to help business owners monitor operations, manage inventory, record sales, control finances, communicate with suppliers, and make better decisions using AI.

Core Features

- Business Dashboard and Action Centre
- Sales and Inventory Management
- Automated Low-Stock Alerts and Reordering
- Supplier and Procurement Management
- Cash Flow and Financial Intelligence
- Customer and Staff Management
- AI Business Chronicle and Event Logging
- Business Health Monitoring and Early Warnings
- Executive Reports and Business Analytics
- Multi-Branch and Multi-Business Support
- Offline-First Data Capture and Synchronization
- Authentication, Security, and Audit Logs
- Backup and Disaster Recovery

Technology Goals

- Secure user authentication
- Supabase database with Row Level Security
- AI integration through a protected backend
- Responsive mobile-first interface
- Android application readiness
